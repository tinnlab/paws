- **FB-8** [status: resolved] — the orchestrator asked that the lesson behind all five regressions be carried out of this feature into the lifecycle skill rather than left in its design doc. → Stated as a reviewable angle:

  **THE RULE — stale-snapshot state.** State captured at time T and consulted at time T+n is a defect generator whenever the user (or another actor) can change the underlying thing in between. Prefer reading the live source at the moment of use. If something genuinely must be remembered, capture it at the moment it is known to be true, and keep whatever pairs with it captured at that same moment.

  **THE INSTANCE.** Voice dictation remembered the composer's caret/selection at record start and consulted it to decide where to write the transcript. Five successive blind audit rounds each found a defect in that one mechanism, and each round's fix added another guard against staleness. The guards could not be completed: for a bare caret the remembered text is `''`, and `value.slice(n, n) === ''` for any in-range `n`, so a wholly replaced draft still "held" — the guard was not merely incomplete but *vacuous*. The loop only ended when the mechanism was deleted (the DOM already holds the live insertion point) and the one genuinely-remembered value — what was replaced, needed for cancel — was moved to first-write time, where it is paired with the span it describes by construction. Round 6 was the first clean round.

  **WHAT TO LOOK FOR IN A DIFF.** A field captured in an `init`/`start`/`open`/`mount` handler and read in a later `tick`/`submit`/`stop`/`cancel` path. Two fields that describe the same thing but are captured at different moments (an offset and the text at that offset; an id and its resolved object; a path and its stat). A guard whose job is to detect that a snapshot went stale — ask what it compares when the snapshot is EMPTY or zero-length, because that is where such guards are usually vacuous rather than merely weak. And the tell that this angle is needed: two or more consecutive fix rounds landing in the same function.

  [generalizable: yes — add "stale-snapshot state" to the phase-6 audit roster as a first-class angle, with the diff heuristics above; a reviewer asking "captured when, used when, and what pairs with it?" would have collapsed six rounds into one.]

## Harvested / generalizable

- FB-1's rule is the one worth folding into the lifecycle: **a finding reported
  against a URL you did not build is not yet evidence about source.** Confirm the
  bundle first (linked asset hashes, and whether the CSS/JS the symptom
  implicates is actually present), then attribute. The counter-case is expensive
  in both directions — either a fabricated "fix" to source that was never broken,
  or a real defect dismissed as stale.
